# Country slides template

**Eight minutes. Six slides maximum. Due at T − 2 weeks.**

The Day 1 morning exchange is where the week gets its raw material. The synthesis
session that follows it, and much of what the Secretariat carries forward into
the Action Plan, is built from what you put on these six slides.

!!! danger "The one instruction that matters"

    **Be candid about what did not work.**

    Every office in the room has a pilot that stalled, a partnership that was
    never signed, a model that was never deployed, a dashboard nobody opened.
    Those are the more useful half of the exchange. A room of six polished success
    stories teaches nobody anything; a room where four offices admit the same
    obstacle has just identified a continental priority.

    The synthesis session at 11:45 is explicitly designed to pool these.

---

## The six slides

**1 · Who you are and what you tried**
Your office, and the one-line version of what you have attempted with AI or
non-traditional data. If you have attempted nothing yet, say that — it is a
legitimate and common position, and knowing how many offices are at that point is
itself information the Action Plan needs.

**2 · The use case**
What statistical problem were you trying to solve? Not the technology — the
problem. "We wanted quarterly subnational activity indicators between censuses"
is a use case. "We wanted to try machine learning" is not.

**3 · What you did**
Data source, method, who did the work, how long it took. Rough numbers are fine.

**4 · What came out**
The result, if there is one. A chart, a table, a screenshot, a URL. If the result
never materialised, this slide says why, and it becomes the most useful slide in
your deck.

**5 · What got in the way**
Access to data, legal framework, cost, skills, infrastructure, staff turnover,
lack of a mandate. Name the actual obstacle rather than the polite version of it.

**6 · What you would want from others**
One concrete thing: a method you would reuse, a partnership you would join, a
question you would like another office to answer. This is the slide that turns
the session into follow-up projects under activities 3.1.1 and 3.1.2.

---

## Format

- **Six slides maximum.** A timekeeper is appointed, and the session is the one
  most likely to overrun.
- Either language. Interpretation is provided.
- Any tool — PowerPoint, Google Slides, PDF. Send a PDF if you want it to look
  the same on the room's machine as on yours.
- Send it to the Secretariat by **T − 2 weeks** so the facilitators can group
  related experiences and prepare the synthesis questions.
